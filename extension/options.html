Monday token

River reads and updates Monday with your personal API token. It stays in this Chrome profile and is only sent to api.monday.com.

  1. In Monday, click your profile picture, then Developers.
  2. Open My access tokens and copy your token.
  3. Paste it below and save.

Ways to open River

  • Alt + Shift + R on any tab, or click the River icon in the toolbar. Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  • Type rv and a space in the address bar, then a client name. Your tasks appear as suggestions.
  • Highlight a name on any page, right-click it and choose Find in River.
